Privacy Policy

At CroSho, we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information as a buyer or seller on our platform. By using CroSho, you consent to the practices described below.

1. Data Collection

We collect the following information when you use CroSho:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, shipping address, and payment details provided during registration or purchases.
  • Account Information: Username, password, and profile details for sellers and buyers.
  • Usage Data: Browsing activity, search history, and interactions with listings or other users.
  • Communication Data: Messages between buyers and sellers, or with CroSho support.

2. Data Usage

We use your data to:

  • Facilitate transactions between buyers and sellers.
  • Provide customer support and resolve disputes.
  • Send transactional emails (e.g., order confirmations) and promotional updates (with your consent).
  • Improve our platform through analytics and user feedback.

3. Data Sharing

We may share your data with:

  • Sellers/Buyers: Sharing buyer shipping information with sellers to fulfill orders.
  • Service Providers: Third-party payment processors, shipping services, and analytics providers.
  • Legal Authorities: If required by law or to protect CroSho’s rights.
We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations).
  • Opt out of promotional emails at any time.
To exercise these rights, contact us at support@crosho.com.

5. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption for payment information and secure servers for data storage. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies and Tracking

CroSho uses cookies to enhance your experience, such as remembering your preferences and analyzing site usage. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings, though disabling cookies may limit functionality.

7. Changes to the Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page, and we’ll notify you via email or a notice on the platform. Continued use of CroSho after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
