Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common crochet queries

Once your handmade item ships, you'll receive an email with tracking information. Most domestic orders arrive within 5-7 business days. International shipping times vary by location.

Can't find your tracking email? Check your spam folder or contact us with your order number.

Many artisans accept custom color requests! Look for the "Custom Orders Available" badge on product pages or contact the seller directly through their profile.

For best results, include color swatches or Pantone numbers when making special requests.

We'd love to have you join our artisan community! Simply register as a seller and complete your artisan profile to start showcasing your crochet creations.

Our team reviews all new seller applications within 2 business days to ensure quality standards.

Due to the handmade nature of our products, we offer returns within 14 days for damaged or defective items. Custom orders are final sale.

To initiate a return, please contact us with your order number and photos of the issue. We'll work with the artisan to resolve your concern.

Our artisans work with a variety of yarns including:

  • Premium acrylics for durability
  • Natural cotton for softness
  • Eco-friendly bamboo blends
  • Luxury wool and alpaca fibers
  • Organic and sustainable options

Each product listing specifies the materials used - look for the "Materials" section on product pages.
