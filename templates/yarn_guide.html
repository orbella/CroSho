Yarn Guide

Choosing the right yarn is key to a successful crochet project. This guide covers yarn weights, types, and their best uses.

Yarn Weights

Weight Name Hook Size Best For
0 Lace 1.5-2.25 mm Delicate doilies, lace shawls
1 Fingering 2.25-3.25 mm Socks, lightweight scarves
4 Worsted 5.5-6.5 mm Blankets, sweaters, hats
6 Super Bulky 9-15 mm Chunky scarves, cozy rugs

Yarn Types

Acrylic: Affordable, durable, and easy to wash. Great for beginners.

Cotton: Soft and breathable, perfect for summer garments and dishcloths.

Wool: Warm and elastic, ideal for winter wear like sweaters and mittens.
